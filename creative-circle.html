how it works

From first hello to your first workshop.

  1. Tell us about your venue

    Share the kind of space you run, the people you want to bring together and your preferred timing.

  2. Shape your calendar with us

    We’ll discuss the right mix of experiences and how they could fit your venue across the year.

  3. Host your community

    Vijyaz Studio handles artistic planning and facilitation, and your venue welcomes participants. We confirm event logistics together as part of the proposal.
