before you ask

Good to know.

I can't draw. Is this still for me?

Yes, and you're exactly who the studio was opened for. No experience is needed. Vijya starts from wherever you are, and most people surprise themselves.

Where does a 1-on-1 session happen?

At the studio in Jaipur, or in your own space if that's where you're most comfortable. Tell us which you'd prefer when you register.

What do I need to bring?

Nothing. All materials are included in a session, and every DIY kit arrives with what you need inside the box.

How much does it cost?

Tell us what you have in mind through the registration form and we'll follow up with the details and pricing for your session or kit.
