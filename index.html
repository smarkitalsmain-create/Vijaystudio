meet the artist

Vijya Laxmi Rathore, the hands behind the studio.

Vijya grew up in Jaipur with colour on every side: Rajasthani textiles, folk art, and a city that never stops making things.

She taught herself every medium she works in, from ink portraits on old newspaper to acrylic on canvas and clay. Her pieces hang in living rooms and exhibitions, and many of them found their owners through a friend of a friend.

“She doesn't teach you to paint. She takes you back to before you decided you couldn't.”

She opened Vijyaz Studio in 2023 for people who would never call themselves artists, because she doesn't think anyone should need the title to begin. She still leads every session herself.
